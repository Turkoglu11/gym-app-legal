Delete your account

PackLift · developer: xh87 (Bojan Milanovic)

You can delete your PackLift account and all data linked to it at any time.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the app and tap your initials (top right on Home) to open Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Manage account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

You need to be online. The account is deleted right away.

Option 2: by email (if you no longer have the app)

Write to hidobojan11@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email. We may ask you to confirm the request so that nobody else can delete your account.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and password).
  • Your profile: name or nickname, training profile, age confirmation and consent dates.
  • All backed-up workouts, sets, weekly goals and body weight entries.

Data is removed from our active database immediately; any copies in backups are gone within 30 days. Nothing is kept.

What stays on your phone

Workouts saved on your phone are not deleted with the account, so the app keeps working offline. To remove them too, uninstall the app (or clear its data in your phone's settings).
