Privacy Policy

Gym Progress Tracker by Hido · Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Gym Progress Tracker app ("the app") collects, why, where it is stored, and how you can delete it. We keep it short and only collect what the app needs to work.

Who is responsible

The app is made by xh87 (xh87.app), run by Bojan Milanovic (individual developer), Serbia. Bojan Milanovic is the data controller.
Contact for anything about privacy: hidobojan11@gmail.com

The short version

  • You can use the app without an account. Then your data stays only on your phone.
  • If you create an account, your workouts are backed up so you can use them on another phone.
  • Body weight is only saved if you agree first, and you can delete it at any time.
  • No ads, no advertising trackers, no selling of your data.
  • You can delete your account and all its data inside the app.

We do not ask for diagnoses or medical information. The app gives general training information only; it is not medical advice.

What we do not collect

  • No location, contacts, photos or microphone access.
  • No advertising ID and no ads.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. We use one service provider to run the account and backup feature: Supabase (database and sign-in). It processes data only on our behalf. Its servers may be outside Serbia; such transfers are covered by the provider's data protection terms.

Share cards: when you tap Share, the app creates an image on your phone and hands it to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp or Instagram). We do not receive or store these images.

Friends: if you add friends (with an invite code), each friend sees your name, your weekly-goal streak, how many workouts you did in the last 7 days, and the name and exercises of your last workout. Friends never see your weights, body weight, email or anything else. You can remove or block a friend at any time. If you report someone, we review the report; the reported person is not told who reported them.

Feedback emails: if you send us feedback, we receive your email address and what you write, including the app version and device type the app adds to the message. We use it only to answer you and fix problems.

Legal basis

  • Providing the app and the account you asked for (contract).
  • Your consent for body weight data. You can withdraw it at any time in the app (Progress → Body → Delete all body weight data).
  • Legal obligations, such as the minimum age.

How long we keep data

Data in your account is kept until you delete it or delete your account. Data only on your phone is removed when you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. When you delete your account, your data is removed from our active database immediately; any copies in backups are gone within 30 days.

Your rights

Under the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection (and the GDPR where it applies) you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, object to or limit its use, and withdraw consent. Most of this you can do directly in the app; for anything else write to hidobojan11@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days. You can also complain to the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection of Serbia (www.poverenik.rs).

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Account → Manage account → Delete account. You can also ask by email. See Delete your account for details.

Children

The app is not meant for children. You must be 16 or older to create an account.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). In our database, each user can only access their own data. No system is perfect, but we work to protect your data and fix problems quickly.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
